How it works

From data chaos to clarity

Three simple steps to an AI that truly understands your life.

Connect your sources

Link your email, calendar, finance accounts, photos, and any other service using our secure connectors. Setup takes minutes.

02

Build your knowledge graph

OxagenAI automatically extracts entities, events, and relationships from your data and weaves them into your personal knowledge graph.
